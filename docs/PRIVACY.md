# Privacy and test-data policy

The repository accepts synthetic fixtures only.

Do not commit:

- raw IRC logs or archives;
- real nicknames, account names or hostmasks from research material;
- private messages or copied conversation excerpts;
- runtime credentials or service configuration;
- database snapshots or generated player exports.

Behavioral tests must replace real identities and prose with minimal synthetic
examples while preserving only the state transition being tested.
